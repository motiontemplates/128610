How it works

  • Declared runtime profile: html-canvas/1.
  • Editable inputs are mapped through the template parameter schema and preset to its existing animation project.
  • The declared duration policy follows the current content; the package plan determines final timing.

The documented output is 1920 × 1080, 60 fps. The public demo is 57 seconds. Use the actual package parameter bounds; extra materials do not automatically create extra scenes. Stretching, speeding up or partially cutting a native scene, changing the engine or switching to portrait. Declaring arbitrary copy length or every product topic suitable without actual layout and semantic review.

Inputs and materials

  • Required · The current brand name, logo and applicable feature icons.
  • Required · Your topic, script and facts to preserve.
  • Required · Current product screenshots, screen recordings and display materials for the declared media slots.
  • Optional · A licensed music track or your preference for music in this production.
  • Optional · The current voice recording or your narration preference for this production.
